# Hi, I'm Mandi

I lead product and design for complex products and growing teams: finding what matters, building it well, and learning from what ships. More at [mandiobrien.com](https://www.mandiobrien.com).

Recently I've extended that into hands-on product building, taking working software from idea to production for real communities and occasions in my life. I set the product direction, design the experience, write the specs, make the product and technical tradeoffs, and test, debug and decide what ships. I use AI coding agents as implementation partners; they write most of the code.

### Tab-Splits
Turns a restaurant receipt into a fair, itemized tab for everyone at the table, with no app or account for guests. Built for the dinners I host with Amuse-Bouche, a Denver food collective.
[tabsplits.com](https://www.tabsplits.com) · [repo](https://github.com/mandirao/tabsplits)

### DenverFeeds
Three curated Denver event feeds, one each for indie music, food, and arts and sciences, serving the Meetup communities I organize. AI drafts listings; a person reviews every one before it publishes.
[Setlist Social Feed](https://www.setlistsocialfeed.com) · [Amuse-Bouche Insider](https://amuseboucheinsider.com) · [Artistry/Nerdistry Live](https://artistrynerdistry.com) · [repo](https://github.com/mandirao/DenverFeeds)

### Fat Bear Week HQ
A party app for one real Fat Bear Week watch party: invites, RSVPs, a potluck sheet and a bear field guide, then a live bracket game played on phones and the TV. Built for about 20 friends and one deadline; now a read-only archive of the night.
[fatbear.sprigbo.com](https://fatbear.sprigbo.com) · [repo](https://github.com/mandirao/fatbearweek-party)

Each repo's README covers the problem, the key decisions and tradeoffs, and how it was built.
